# Amstrad PC9486i and PC9555i catalogue evidence

The catalogue groups these desktops by their joint CeBIT 1995 presentation.
Spanish is the source text for the English, French, Italian and Portuguese
translations.

| Claim | Evidence | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PC9486i with a 66 MHz 486DX2 or a 100 MHz 486 | [Computerwoche, 7 April 1995](https://www.computerwoche.de/article/2738334/pentium-und-486-rechner-vorgestellt-amstrad-will-auch-weiterhin-in-dem-pc-geschaeft-mitmischen.html) | The report records availability at the time. The 100 MHz processor's exact designation remains open. |
| PC9555i with 75, 90 or 100 MHz Pentium, 8 MB RAM, 256 KB cache, DOS 6.2 and Windows 3.11 | Same CeBIT report | Announced configurations. |
| PCI and two Enhanced IDE interfaces on both models | Same CeBIT report | Motherboard revision and chip identities remain open. |
| SVGA on both models and distinct AT and AT/PCI entries for PC9486 and PC9486i | [Cliff Lawson, General notes on Amstrad PCs](https://www.freetimeweb.nl/home/computer/alt/lawsons-amstrad-computer_site/web.ukonline.co.uk/cliff.lawson/general.htm) | Original Amstrad support summary. |
| PC9486i manual in Amstrad's support library in 1997 | [Cliff Lawson, reply to Roger Green, 15 September 1997](https://www.freetimeweb.nl/home/computer/alt/lawsons-amstrad-computer_site/web.ukonline.co.uk/cliff.lawson/quest5.htm) | The reply establishes historical custody. A copy is still sought. |
| PC9555i P75 Premier £949, P75 Multimedia £1,099 and P100 Multimedia £1,169 | [Amstrad Direct advertisement, The Times, 23 May 1996, preserved by ACME](https://acpc.me/) | UK advertised prices. The Multimedia offers included 4× CD-ROM, 16-bit sound and stereo speakers. |
| PC9555i owner reports P120 and onboard S3 Trio. Lawson describes the video-disable jumper in his own unit. | [Cliff Lawson, Q0222, 16 November 1997](https://www.freetimeweb.nl/home/computer/alt/lawsons-amstrad-computer_site/web.ukonline.co.uk/cliff.lawson/quest6.htm#Q0222) | Individual units. The P120 report is kept alongside the published CeBIT configurations. |
| S154P VIO Rev. 1.1 board marking | [Jon Smeed, Amstrad support archive, 1 March 1999](https://www.freetimeweb.nl/home/computer/alt/lawsons-amstrad-computer_site/web.ukonline.co.uk/cliff.lawson/queste.htm) | Owner transcription. The SI54P reading and OEM relationship await physical confirmation. |
| PC9555i firmware candidate and related Epson revisions | [Archived Epson FTP and the 1.9X and 2.0X bulletins](https://archive.org/details/EpsonFTP), with the static comparisons preserved in the library | The owner assigned the Epson/ECS candidate provisionally. The Epson packages target the ActionTower 8100/8200. PC9555i compatibility and boot validation are pending. |

The original sources and firmware evidence are tracked in the local library.
Both sheets have research status. A photograph with documented publication
rights is pending for each model.
