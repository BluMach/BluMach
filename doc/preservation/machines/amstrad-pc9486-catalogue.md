# Amstrad PC9486 catalogue evidence

The PC9486 is listed under the 1994 PC9000 range. Spanish is the source text
for the English, French, Italian and Portuguese translations.

| Claim | Source and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Introduction in early 1994, professional range, home multimedia offer, production in England | [Computerwoche, 14 January 1994](https://www.computerwoche.de/article/2761130/amstrad-steigt-wieder-in-den-profimarkt-ein.html). The article rounds the British prices to £850 and £1,250. |
| British prices from £849.99 and £1,249.99, 25 MHz SX and 50/66 MHz DX2, 4 MB RAM, 1 MB VRAM and 130/214/340 MB disks | [BYTE, May 1994, Pentium OverDrive-ready PCs](https://archive.org/details/eu_BYTE-1994-05_OCR). Consulted the archived text. The prices describe configurations available at the time. |
| Upgrades and first-time buyers, processor socket, tutorials, 64 MB published memory limit | [PC Review 30, April 1994, Amstrad PC9000 Multimedia and Amstrad 9486 (66MHz, 486DX2)](https://archive.org/details/pc-review-030). Consulted the archived text, including David Hennell’s comments and the specification box. |
| Cirrus Logic 5426 local-bus video, 48 MB published memory limit, five ISA slots and 1.44 MB floppy | [Joystick 47, March 1994, Les PC 9000 d’Amstrad](https://archive.org/details/joystick047). Consulted the archived text and identified the DX2-50 test configuration. The sheet retains both published RAM limits. |
| Distinct AT and AT/PCI entries for the PC9486 and PC9486i | [Cliff Lawson, General notes on Amstrad PCs](https://www.freetimeweb.nl/home/computer/alt/lawsons-amstrad-computer_site/web.ukonline.co.uk/cliff.lawson/general.htm). |
| UX486VIO-A Rev. 1.0 and UMC UM82C481BF/UM82C482AF in one unit | [Zalog’s PC9486 owner report, April 2021](https://www.vogons.org/viewtopic.php?p=953066#p953066). Board OEM and production revisions remain open. |
| 64 KiB system image and 256 KiB memory capture with a four-byte reset-vector difference at FFF1–FFF4 | Same owner report and static comparison of the two preserved attachments. The standalone image contains the PC9486 identity and the date 11/11/92. The memory capture contains separate option ROMs and its final 64 KiB supply the second system block for comparison. Boot and peripheral validation remain pending. |

The [Centre for Computing History](https://www.computinghistory.org.uk/det/24997/Amstrad-PC9486-Series/)
catalogues the January 1994 Amstrad brochure as CH24997. A complete copy and
a photograph with documented publication rights are pending.

Source copies and hashes are tracked in the local library. The sheet has
research status. The surviving BIOS identifies the machine, and resolving the
reset-vector discrepancy and mapping board resources are prerequisites for a
faithful implementation.
