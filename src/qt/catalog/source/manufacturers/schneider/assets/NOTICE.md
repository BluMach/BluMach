# Schneider Computer Division historical logo

This mark identifies the historical manufacturer in BluMach's collection.
BluMach is independent and has no affiliation with or endorsement from Schneider.

[Wikimedia Commons source and rights statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schneider_Computer_Division_logo_(horizontal).svg)

Takimata reconstructed this version from a Schneider advertisement. Commons
classifies the design as PD-textlogo. Trademark rights remain with their
respective holders. The asset retains its own status separately from BluMach's
code licence.

The bundled PNG is a full-size rendition of the SVG at a height of 224 pixels,
made with `rsvg-convert --height 224 --keep-aspect-ratio`. The white backing
is supplied by the interface for legibility in light and dark palettes.
